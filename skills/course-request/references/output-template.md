# Request brief and action map

Use this structure for the finished brief. Keep early questions and recommendations shorter. Omit fields that do not apply; mark missing facts instead of filling them in. If no learning is recommended, omit the action map.

## Request

- **Asked for:**
- **Requester:** role
- **Audience and setting:** roles, experience, work context, and relevant constraints
- **Decision-maker:** role, or to confirm
- **Prompting evidence:** supplied sources or "none supplied"

## Goal and performance

- **Business or operational goal:**
- **Required learner performance:**
- **Measure:** signal, baseline, target, and timeframe; mark each unknown
- **Source and access:** available source, or proposed source to confirm
- **Who could check:** role, with responsibility unconfirmed unless accepted

## Diagnosis

| Required action | Obstacle | Evidence or unvalidated assumption | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|

**Existing coverage:** resources checked, findings, or "not checked."

## Recommendation

**Decision status:** proposed / confirmed / deferred

| Response | Why it fits this audience and evidence | Proposed owner and confirmation status | Dependencies or next check |
|---|---|---|---|

**Smaller option considered:** include when recommending a course.

**Unvalidated assumptions and open questions:** only those that could change the response, scope, or next action. Name how each can be checked.

## Action map

**Map status:** draft for review / confirmed by the decision-maker

**Primary goal and audience:**

| Critical job action | Obstacle and evidence status | Practice | Success criterion and feedback | Minimum information | Non-training dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Application:** real task the learner should be able to complete
- **Opportunity to apply:** next realistic situation or work cycle
- **Prerequisites and access:**
- **Reusable assets:** supplied or checked assets; otherwise not identified

## Handoff

- **Next action and proposed owner:**
- **Still awaiting confirmation:**
- **Destination or board:** include only when one was used

Confirmation records approval of this recommendation or map. It does not establish that other teams accepted work or that the performance problem is resolved.

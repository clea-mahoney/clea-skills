# Lesson eval

Check a lesson draft in one pass and get a short list of problems to fix or review. A person makes the final calls.

**[Download the skill (.zip)](https://github.com/clea-mahoney/clea-skills/raw/main/downloads/lesson-eval.zip)** · [Read the skill instructions](SKILL.md)

## Try the walkthrough

[Open the interactive walkthrough](https://clea-mahoney.github.io/clea-skills/docs/walkthrough/) to inspect a fictional lesson, compare the old and new checks, sort the findings, and see the rewrite.

## Use it on your own work

Download the packaged skill, then follow the [setup instructions](SKILL.md#customize-before-you-use-it). Add your company and learner context and replace the voice examples with your own. The skill uses [four checks](evals.md) and reports only what needs attention. It does not rewrite the lesson.

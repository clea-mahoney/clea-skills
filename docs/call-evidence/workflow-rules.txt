# Weekly customer-evidence workflow

These rules describe the original workflow behind this fictional work example. They are reconstructed from the documented skill and Clea's recollection, not a verbatim copy of the former work system.

## Development

Clea began by processing transcripts in Notion AI and adding verified findings to a Notion database. She created a Notion agent to repeat the call-processing workflow, then carried the workflow into a Claude Code skill. The evidence and human-review rules apply across those tools.

## Inputs

Read the new customer-call transcripts and the existing Notion theme register. The presentation follows one week with Tuesday and Thursday calls and a Friday review.

## Evidence

Use verbatim transcripts for quotes, speakers, and timestamps. Automated summaries can locate a moment but cannot establish who said it.

Count each distinct, full-name external customer once per theme, even when their company is unknown. Exclude internal staff and unconfirmed identities from customer counts. Flag missing information without discarding valid evidence.

Preserve risky habits and deliberate caution when they reveal a teachable skill or judgment. A rough early demo by itself does not establish a skills gap.

## Compare with existing research

Check the existing themes before proposing another one. Identify whether the new evidence adds an example, suggests a subtheme, relates to another theme, or supports a distinct theme.

Add routine, source-linked evidence to an established theme. Leave new themes, changes to the hierarchy, and uncertain matches for human review.

## Decide what education can address

Distinguish teachable gaps from product bugs, feature requests, work already in progress, deliberate Holds, and situations where no education applies. A larger speaker count does not make a product issue an education priority.

Keep a topic on Hold when the agreed plan is to wait. Do not create education resources for a migration while the migration direction is still being worked out.

Treat an uncertain cause as a question. A report of a timeout does not prove that the behavior is a defect or that a learning resource is the right response.

## Friday laptop alert

At the weekly run, prepare a summary of the calls, the evidence added, proposed themes and overlaps, product reports, and questions requiring a person. The laptop alert offered two options: continue in Claude Code or copy the questions and quotes to use with the skill elsewhere. Clea checked the related Notion rows before recording a decision. This sample shows two calls reviewed and four questions.

For each question, preserve the supporting quote and timestamp, possible existing theme matches, and what still needs checking. Record Clea's decision without inventing an identity, product diagnosis, or curriculum outcome.

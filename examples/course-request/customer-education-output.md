# Customer education: example draft output

Authored illustration of a full draft, not a recorded model test or an approved decision. All scenario evidence comes from the fictional companion input. The user requested a complete draft with decisions pending.

## Request

- **Asked for:** a custom-reporting course because customers are making report errors.
- **Requester:** Product.
- **Audience and setting:** customer operations coordinators who build custom staffing reports; report readers may need a separate response.
- **Decision-maker:** customer education lead, for the education response.
- **Prompting evidence:** six supplied case summaries, current article description, supplied catalog check, and reported product stability.

## Goal and performance

- **Operational goal:** fewer custom staffing reports needing correction before use.
- **Required learner performance:** select filters that answer the staffing question and validate the report before sharing it.
- **Measure:** reporting cases involving incorrect filters or unchecked copied settings, reviewed over an agreed period. Baseline, target, and period are to confirm. Case counts alone may change with usage or reporting habits.
- **Source and access:** Support can review cases. This is a limited signal, not a measure of all customer reports or staffing-review quality.
- **Who could check:** Support, proposed responsibility unconfirmed.

## Diagnosis

| Required action | Obstacle | Evidence or unvalidated assumption | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select the relevant locations | All-location default and unclear inactive status | Two supplied cases; prevalence unknown | Ask Product to review the default and summary; provide an interim check |
| Choose the date field that answers the question | Difficulty interpreting date-field meaning | Two supplied cases | Practice choosing a field and explaining the choice |
| Validate a copied report before sharing | Old date range carried forward; reason for missed check unknown | One supplied case | Add a validation aid; test whether practice is also needed |
| Create a report | Missing permission | One supplied case resolved by access change | Provide the access route; no demonstrated skill gap in this case |

**Existing coverage:** the supplied catalog check found no reporting course. The help article covers filter location but lacks a worked staffing example and validation aid. Its exact content has not been independently inspected.

## Recommendation

**Decision status:** proposed, awaiting the education lead's confirmation.

| Response | Why it fits | Proposed owner and confirmation status | Dependencies or next check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot a short optional practice session for report builders | Two cases suggest a date-field judgment gap; a small pilot can check the need before a course build | Education, pending approval | Invite builders and observe choices in the test workspace |
| Add a worked staffing example and validation checklist to the article | People need help checking settings while building or reusing a report | Documentation, unconfirmed | Confirm article changes and access guidance |
| Review location defaults and inactive-location visibility | Two cases point to a tool obstacle that practice alone would leave in place | Product, unconfirmed | Confirm intended behavior and feasible changes |
| Clarify the permission request route | The access case was resolved without training | Documentation and the customer administrator role, unconfirmed | Confirm the actual request process before writing guidance |

A full course is not justified by these cases alone. Start with the smaller practice pilot and point-of-use support, then review whether the pattern warrants a reusable learning experience. No manager assignment, coaching program, or customer-success work is assumed.

**Open questions:** How often do these errors occur? Would builders choose correctly with clearer field guidance alone? What do report readers need to check? Education can seek voluntary examples or interviews; no customer data access is assumed.

## Action map

**Map status:** speculative draft for review. Detailed mapping would normally follow confirmation of the recommendation.

**Primary goal and audience:** help report builders produce staffing reports that answer the intended question and pass a validation check.

| Critical job action | Obstacle and evidence status | Practice | Success criterion and feedback | Minimum information | Non-training dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Choose a date field and range for a staffing question | Interpretation gap suggested by two cases | Given a staffing question and fictional shift records, choose filters and explain the choice | Results include shifts occurring in the specified month; feedback traces wrongly included records to the selected date field | Meaning of the relevant date fields and the staffing question | Verified field definitions and test data |
| Select locations relevant to the question | Confusing default suggested by two cases | Check and correct a report that includes inactive locations | Included locations match the question; feedback identifies each unintended location | Location-status definitions and checklist | Product review of defaults and status visibility |
| Validate a copied report before sharing | A missed check is observed; underlying cause unconfirmed | Inspect a copied report with stale settings and compare it with checked totals | Detect stale dates and location scope, correct them, and explain the validation | Short validation checklist | Confirm a realistic validation method with Product |

- **Application:** build and validate a staffing report in the test workspace.
- **Opportunity to apply:** the customer's next monthly staffing-reporting cycle.
- **Prerequisites and access:** familiarity with standard reports and test-workspace access. Confirm creation permission before practice.
- **Reusable assets:** supplied help article and test workspace with invented data and checked totals.

## Handoff

- **Next action:** education lead confirms or changes the recommendation before any pilot or external assignment.
- **Still awaiting confirmation:** other teams' ownership, measurement plan, reader needs, and the final map.
- **Next after map approval:** design the short practice experience; revisit the course decision after the pilot.

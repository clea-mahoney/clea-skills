# Customer education: Product asks for a reporting course

This is an entirely fictional scenario. The organization, observations, and resources were invented for this example. It contains no customer or employer records.

## Brief

I lead customer education at a company that sells scheduling software. Product is asking for a course because customers are messing up custom reports. Customers have not asked us for a reporting course.

The learners are customer operations coordinators who already use standard reports. They need custom reports for their monthly staffing review. Some build reports themselves; others only read reports someone else prepared. We have not checked whether those groups need the same support.

Support has supplied six fictional case summaries for this exercise:

- Two coordinators included inactive locations without realizing it. The report builder defaults to all locations, and the summary does not show which are inactive.
- Two coordinators filtered for the month when a shift was created instead of the month when it occurred. They could find the date fields but could not explain which would answer their staffing question.
- One coordinator used a copied report with a prior month's date range and did not check it before sharing.
- One coordinator could not create a report because their account did not have permission. They could create one after their administrator changed access.

These cases suggest possible causes; they do not tell us how common each cause is across customers.

We have a current help article that explains where the filters are. It has no worked staffing example or report-validation checklist. There is no existing reporting course in the catalog I supplied. A test workspace is available with invented scheduling data and checked totals. Product says the filter behavior is stable for the next release.

We want fewer reports that need correction before staffing reviews. We do not yet have a baseline, target, or measurement period. Support can review reporting cases, but we cannot inspect customers' staffing reviews or measure every report they create.

Education can offer optional online practice. Product owns the report builder, and the documentation team owns the help article. I can propose work to those teams, but they have not accepted it. Customer success cannot support a new program this quarter. We cannot assign work to customers' managers or require customer coaching.

I can approve the education recommendation. Please help me decide whether a course is warranted and what else needs to happen. Keep the output in chat.
